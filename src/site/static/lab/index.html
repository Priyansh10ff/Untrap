The best ultralight backpacks of 2026, tested on 600 trail miles

By Maya Torres · 12 min read

We carried four frameless and framed ultralight packs on the Colorado Trail for six weeks. Each pack hauled between 7 and 14 kilograms through rain, scree and a lot of resupply boxes. Here's what held up.

The Arc Haul is still the pack to beat for long carries. The adjustable frame let us move weight onto the hips on climbs, and the roll-top swallowed a bear canister without complaint. At 960 grams it isn't the lightest, but it carried 14 kilograms more comfortably than anything else here.
